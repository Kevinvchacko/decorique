Questions

      

        
How far ahead should I book?
At least 3 days for decor and a week for gifts. For a weekend birthday, book earlier. We take a limited number of setups each week so every one gets full attention.

        
What if I need to cancel?
The 50% advance is refundable until 48 hours before the date. After that we've already bought the materials, so it isn't.

        
Can I pick my own theme?
Yes, that's the point. Tell us what they love and we design around it. A custom theme costs the same as one of ours.

        
Do you work outside Chennai?
Decor is Chennai only for now. Gifts will ship across India soon. Message us and we'll tell you what's possible for your city.

        
My budget is smaller. What can you do?
Tell us the number. A single wall, a polaroid set or a chart board can still make the day, and we'll say plainly what fits.
